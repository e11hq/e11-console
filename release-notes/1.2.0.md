## A new look
- A cleaner, calmer design on computers and phones, in both dark and light mode (switch with the sun/moon button, or Me → Light mode on a phone)
- A new startup screen that ticks off your projects, tasks and Inbox as they load, with your own numbers

## E11 Console on your iPhone
- Open console.e11hq.com in Safari, tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**, and it works like an app
- Built for the phone: My Day, My Tasks, Inbox and Projects tabs, a + button for new tasks, and swipe gestures (swipe from the left edge to go back, swipe between tabs)
- Turn on **Notifications on this phone** in the Me tab to get alerts even when the app is closed

## Smarter search
- Search understands projects, clients, people, tags, descriptions and comments, and forgives small typos
- Try words like “overdue”, “today”, “urgent” or “mine”
- Client Assets search forgives typos too

## Trash
- Deleted tasks, projects, comments and files go to the Trash instead of disappearing (Admins and Account Managers can see it and restore things)
- Items stay in the Trash for 30 days; finished tasks move there 30 days after they’re completed

## Mac app
- Stays connected when the window is minimised, instead of showing “Offline”
- To get this, run the install line once more: open **Terminal**, paste `curl -fsSL https://console.e11hq.com/install | bash` and press Return

## Fixes
- Comment box is taller, and the “Replying to” strip fits inside it
- People who can’t edit a description now see it as plain text
- File previews on phones show the file name and size clearly
