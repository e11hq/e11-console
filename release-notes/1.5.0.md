## New: Content calendar
- A new Content calendar in the sidebar for Social, Account Managers and Admins
- Month view, and Week view by client
- Shows every task that has a channel
- Tasks have new Channel, Go-live and Occasion fields
- Filter by several clients and several channels at once
- The occasions panel lists what's coming up and how ready each client is. Plan creates one task per client
- Admins and Account Managers can add occasions and correct estimated dates
- On the phone, the calendar is a list of the next 6 weeks

## Occasion dates confirmed automatically
- Twice a day the app reads the official UAE holiday calendar and confirms the dates for Ramadan, the Eids, Arafat Day, Islamic New Year and the Prophet's Birthday
- A date someone corrected by hand is never changed
- When a date changes, Admins and Account Managers get an alert, and posts still planned for the old date are pointed out
- Occasions work for every year ahead

## Greetings
- On occasions, the startup screen and Home greet you accordingly: Ramadan Kareem, Eid Mubarak, Merry Christmas, Happy New Year and more
- For UAE National Day it says "Eshi Biladi", from 2 to 15 December

## Also
- A glass edge on the Mac Dock icon, like other macOS apps. A custom Dock icon gets one too
- Red buttons such as Roll back and Delete are red again on the Mac and desktop
- Phones always load the newest version of every part of the app
