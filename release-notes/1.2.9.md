## New Home page
- **Account Managers and Admins:**
  - Cards for tasks due today, tasks your team finished this week, and tasks waiting for CD review
  - **Your created tasks** (Admins: **Team's created tasks**) with Overdue, Today, This week and All open tabs
  - A month chart of finished work, a week Schedule, a Projects card, checklist items, Recent activity and Team on time
- **Production, Social and everyone else:**
  - **Up next:** the task to work on now, with its checklist and a Submit files / Mark my part done button
  - **My pipeline:** your open tasks grouped by Task Update
  - **My week:** your tasks on their due and start dates
  - Recent activity, your CD reviews, Amendments to fix, Latest files and your on-time streak
- **See all** and the top cards open My Tasks showing the same tasks, with the matching filters already set

## My Tasks
- New filters: **Due date**, **Finished in the last 7 days** and **Waiting for CD review**
- Admins can pick **All Account Managers & Admins**
- Opening My Tasks from the sidebar always starts on To do, with no filters

## Everyone
- The sidebar has no page title under the logo. Search and New task sit on the logo row.
- A new task closed without anything filled in isn't kept
- In the Sidebar task view, the Activity and Comments icons work from inside a comment thread
- An open task no longer reloads when you move between pages
- An Inbox update about files sent for review opens Review Requests and highlights that submission

## Phone
- PDFs open as pages that fit the screen. Word, PowerPoint and Excel files open the same way once the server has the Office preview update.
- Review requests show a preview of PDF and Office files
