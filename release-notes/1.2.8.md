## Finance
- A new **Finance** section with a Dashboard, Projects and Expenses:
  - **Dashboard:** counts per Finance status with this month vs last month, a monthly graph, the status mix, what's invoiced by client, and recent changes
  - **Projects:** every task's Finance status
  - **Expenses:** this month's spending, cash flow, subscriptions coming up, and every transaction (filter by month)
- New roles for Finance-only people: **Finance only · Admin** and **Finance only · Viewer**. They see only the Finance pages, on a computer.
- Anyone can also be given **Finance Admin** or **Finance Viewer** access on top of their role (Team page ••• menu). Finance Admins add monthly subscriptions, staff reimbursements and extra expenses, with invoices.
- Any role can also be given **Admin rights** on top of their role.

## Task page
- New **Fields** under the description:
  - **Task Update** (WIP, Shoot, Amendments…): everyone on the task is notified when it changes
  - **Finance** status: Account Managers and Admins only
  - **Guardian**
- New **date picker** for start and due dates (computers)
- In the Sidebar view, **Activity** has its own icon under Comments
- Files sent for CD review stay out of Attachments until the CD approves them

## Account Managers and Admins
- Home shows **Your created tasks**
- **My Tasks** lists the tasks you created, with filters: Created date, People, Assigned to me (Admins also get Account managers)
- My Day shows your created tasks. For Admins it's **E11 Day**, with every Account Manager's tasks.

## Everyone
- **Automatic light/dark:** light from sunrise, dark from sunset (Dubai). Choose Light, Dark or Auto in **My profile → Appearance**, or in the phone's Me tab.
- The Inbox always opens on your unread updates
- My profile is laid out in full-width cards
- Mac app: the update bar sits at the bottom of the window

## Phone
- Pop-up menus darken the page behind them and stop it scrolling
- The assignee list scrolls straight away, and picking people no longer opens the keyboard
- The priority pop-up uses the full width
- Swiping between To Do/Done and Unread/All/Mentions keeps the tabs in place
