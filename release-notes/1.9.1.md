## Phone
- Touch and hold a file or folder in Client Assets, or an attachment in a task, for its options in a sheet (no small buttons on the pictures)
- Each file and folder card is clean: no ⋯ or ⓘ buttons, and no lift when tapped
- A task's Details and Comments each keep their place when switching between them, and swiping between them works over the attachments too
- Comments: the count sits in a round badge; holding a comment no longer brings up the text magnifier
- A long description's Collapse button stays in reach while reading, and expanding or collapsing no longer moves the page
- Tapping an empty description always opens it for typing
- A task's empty fields stay hidden until Show is pressed
- A task's dates sit on two lines, Start and Due, lined up, with an × that's easy to tap
- The date wheel no longer fills in today by itself, and choosing today works
- The box being typed in stays in sight above the keyboard
- A message card at the top swipes away to the right or up
- Content plans: the month and the count each on their own line; "Back to November" says where it goes; the post's client and month sit next to the ×
- Menus show in full, with no doubled lines or empty rows

## Comments
- A link on its own line shows its preview card in its place
- YouTube and Vimeo cards show the video's title, channel and picture

## Everywhere
- Downloading a single file saves the file itself, not a .zip
- Dropping a picture dragged from the page back onto it no longer uploads it again
- The fade above a long description's Expand matches the background
