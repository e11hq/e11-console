## Office Admin
- New role: Office Admin. Admin rights without the task app: their own Home, Inbox with Client Assets and Finance updates, Client Assets, Team and App updates
- Their Home shows Clients, Unread, Team and Claims to approve, with the latest in Client Assets, the Inbox and this month's Finance
- Super Admins and Office Admins see Finance only with Finance Admin or Finance Viewer access, which they can give themselves
- Finance Admins can mark their own claims HR approved and paid

## CD review
- A task waiting for the CD can't move past Review or be completed until the CD approves, for everyone, Admins included. Locked statuses show a lock
- Completing a CD-approved task files its approved files in Client Assets › the client › Files › the task's name. Completing it again replaces them with the newest
- Client approved button for the client's Account Managers and Admins

## Client Assets
- Files holds folders up to 5 levels deep, with a New folder card
- New folders on a client's main page only by Super Admins, Office Admins and Account Managers. Everyone can still open, download and add files there
- Info on every file and folder: who added it, when, size, and for final files who approved them
- A deleted folder goes to the Trash with everything inside it and comes back in one go
- The title bar shows the whole folder path, shortened with … when it doesn't fit

## Seasonal icons and greetings
- The Mac app icon changes for 16 occasions: UAE National Day, Ramadan (the whole month), Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Islamic New Year, Emirati Women's Day, Diwali, Christmas, New Year, and the national days of Pakistan, India, Syria, Lebanon, Armenia, Algeria and South Africa
- Each shows for a week around its day. The startup animation takes on the same look on every device
- On the day itself, a greeting with confetti shows once after startup

## My profile
- Your country and Your city (optional), shown as City, Country on people's cards
- Your birthday, day and month only (optional). On the day you get a Happy birthday, and the rest of the team gets an Inbox update and a notification. Nobody else sees the date

## Mac app
- A new Mac app built for the Mac. You'll see Important update › Update now
- The everyday icon in Apple's new glass look on macOS 26
- Closing the window keeps it running like Slack, so notifications and the Dock badge keep coming. The Dock icon brings it back, ⌘Q quits
- Open at Login and an optional Touch ID lock, in the app menu
- Clicking a notification opens what it's about
