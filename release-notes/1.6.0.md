## Clients
- Projects are now called Clients everywhere, Finance included
- Only Admins add, edit, remove or restore clients. Adding or editing a client opens one pop-up: name, colour, Account Managers (searchable, with an On leave switch for each) and Active / Inactive
- New page for Admins in the name menu: Edit client list, with Active and Inactive tabs
- An inactive client is hidden from everyone, along with its tasks, Inbox updates, Client Assets and Finance history

## Client Assets
- Every new client gets its own folder with Logos, Brand Guidelines, Fonts and Files, linked to the client. Renaming a client renames its folder
- Anyone can add folders and files and remove their own. Account Managers can remove anyone's files and added folders. The main folder and the four starting folders are Admin only. Only an Admin, or whoever made a folder, can rename it
- Freelancers can view and download only
- Adding or removing a file notifies everyone on that client's tasks, its Account Managers and all Admins
- The first image in Logos becomes the client's logo unless an Admin chose one, and the sidebar shows it. A logo chosen with Add / Change logo is also saved into Logos
- One Download button on files

## Account Managers
- See every task in the clients they look after (old ones too) on Home, My Day and My Tasks. Whoever covers for an AM on leave sees them while covering
- Get an update for every change on their clients' tasks
- Their extra rights apply to their clients' tasks and tasks they created. Other clients' tasks are view-only, with comments
- Sidebar, search, calendar and Client Assets show only their clients
- A replaced or on-leave AM stops getting updates on tasks they created for that client; the new AM gets them

## Assigning and roles
- Only Production, Social, Freelancers and the Creative Director can be assigned. Account Managers and Admins were taken off existing tasks
- Creative Director: the Admin's Home, E11 Day and My Tasks, can change any task, and is notified only about tasks they created, CD reviews and mentions. On new tasks the CD can't be assigned while Needs CD review is on. Shown as Creative Director (or Acting CD) everywhere
- Production, Social and Freelancers can't change start / due dates or rename or remove checklist items; they can tick them. Social can add checklist items and write descriptions

## Inbox and notifications
- Every Inbox update is also a push notification on desktop and phone. New switch in My profile: Notifications on this computer
- A long message slides on hover to show the rest

## Faster as it grows
- The app is sent compressed (about a quarter of the size) and devices keep its files until the next update
- Old Inbox updates are cleared: read ones after 60 days, any after 180. Tasks and comments are never touched
