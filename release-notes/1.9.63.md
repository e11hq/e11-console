## Better
- Reading or unreading updates on the Mac or the website now updates the number on your iPhone's E11 Console icon straight away, without opening the app.
- Notifications you've already read — on any device — are taken off your iPhone's Notification Centre and Lock Screen (with the next iPhone app update).
- Turning a file's or folder's link off or on reaches every open app at once.
- E11 Talk keeps up while it's open: new names, photos, roles and clients from E11 Console show without leaving the app, and link cards update when a link is turned off or on.
