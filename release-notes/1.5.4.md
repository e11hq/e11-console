## Occasion reminders
- 45, 30, 14 and 7 days before every occasion, at 10:30 am, Social, Account Managers and Admins get a reminder with how many clients have posts planned. Reminders that fall on a weekend go out on the Friday before
- Account Managers and Admins: "Plan posts in the Content calendar". Social: "Set the Occasion on your post tasks"
- No reminder once every client has a post planned for the occasion
- On a phone with notifications on, it arrives as a notification. Otherwise the app shows it once. Tapping it opens the Content calendar

## Content calendar
- Social no longer see the Plan and New post buttons, since those create tasks. They can still set Channel, Go-live and Occasion on their tasks
