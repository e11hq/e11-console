## Production and Freelancers see only their own work
- Everywhere in the app (Home, lists, search, calendar, the sidebar and the phone's Clients tab), Production and Freelancers see only tasks they're part of: assigned to (the task, its subtask or the task it sits under), created, Guardian of, or @mentioned in
- They see only the clients those tasks are in, and only those clients' Client Assets. Freelancers keep the clients an Admin gave them
- A task they're added to appears straight away. Taken off it, it goes
- Opening a task link someone sent them, for a task they're not on, shows it view-only: they can read it, open its files and comment
