## Notifications about something deleted
- Tapping or clicking one says what was deleted ("This task was deleted", "This comment was deleted", "This content plan was deleted", "This folder was deleted", "This client was deleted") and opens nothing. A deleted task still takes Account Managers and Admins to the Trash.
- A deleted comment's words no longer show in the Inbox: "This comment was deleted" in their place.
- Mac app Inbox: when what an update is about was deleted, the right side is covered with that note (Go to Trash for a deleted task, Account Managers and Admins).
- A comment's notification still on an iPhone or Mac is replaced quietly by "This comment was deleted" when the comment is deleted.
