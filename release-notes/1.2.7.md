## Share a task
- New **Copy link** button at the top right of a task (next to Attach, Delete and Layout) copies a link to that task
- Anyone on the team who opens the link goes straight to the task — in the **E11 Console Mac app** if they use it, otherwise in the browser
- Safari asks once each time whether to open E11 Console: choose **Allow**
- To get this in the Mac app, press **Update now** on the bar at the top of the app (about 10 seconds)
- Computers only; not on the phone
