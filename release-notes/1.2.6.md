## Mac app: more room
- The app now uses the whole window: the window buttons, collapse sidebar, light/dark and new **back / forward** buttons sit in the top row, next to the page title and search
- Back and forward take you through the pages you've visited (also **⌘[** and **⌘]**)
- Drag the window by any empty part of the top row; double-click it to maximise
- Press **Update now** on the bar at the top of the Mac app to get this (about 10 seconds)

## Tasks
- A task always opens on its **Details** tab
- The side panel is wider and sits flat, without a shadow

## Faster images and client logos
- Images load faster: lists and tiles use smaller copies, and the full image opens when you click it
- Client logos fill their blocks edge to edge. New logos are made square on a background that suits the logo (dark behind light logos, light behind dark ones)

## Office previews
- Word, PowerPoint and Excel files get thumbnails and a full preview (starting after the next server update on the NAS)

## Phone
- "Download all" is removed from CD review requests and Client Assets
