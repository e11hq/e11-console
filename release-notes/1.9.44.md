## Out of Office
- New in My profile: Out of Office. Pick the day you're back, and every notification pauses until 9:00 AM that day: E11 Console on the iPhone, Mac and browser, the morning summary, reminders, and E11 Talk. It turns itself off at 9:00 AM on the day you picked, or you can turn it off sooner.
- Nothing is lost: the Inbox and your chats still collect everything. Your own "Send test" notification still comes through.
- When someone is assigning a task, the people list shows "Out of office until …" under the name of anyone who is away. The iPhone shows it once the next E11 Console build is out; E11 Talk's @ list shows it once the next E11 Talk build is out.
