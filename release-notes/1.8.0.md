## Morning round-up
- Account Managers get a Morning round-up at 9:00, Monday to Friday, in the Inbox and on the phone: overdue, due today, waiting for CD review, amendments sent back, client approval missing and your comments scheduled for today
- Super Admins get the same for the whole team
- Opening it shows each line with its tasks, and Overdue, Due today and Waiting for CD open My Tasks with that filter
- It isn't sent on UAE public holidays
- Switch it off, or send yourself one now, in My profile › Notifications

## Comments
- Read receipts: under a comment with @mentions, everyone on the task sees who has seen it and when
- Link previews: links in a comment show a card with the site, title and picture
- WeTransfer links show the number of files, the size and when the link expires, and warn when it expires soon
- The person who wrote the comment can remove a card; the link stays
- A comment can be edited for 10 minutes after it's sent, with the minutes left shown next to Edit comment. After that it can only be deleted

## Task lock after CD review
- Once a task has gone for CD review, its name, description and client, and its subtasks' names, can't be changed by anyone
- New task from this starts a new task with the same client, people and description, linked to the old one. Closed without any change, it goes away again
- Pin to brief on a comment shows it under the description as a brief update; a click jumps to the comment. Pinned comments can't be edited

## Mac app
- In a task's comments, a button to jump to the latest comment once you scroll up, and a "new comments" pill when comments arrive meanwhile
