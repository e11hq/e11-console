## Mac app updates in one click
- When the Mac app needs updating, a bar at the top of the app says so — press **Update now** and it does everything for you: Terminal opens, the app is updated and reopens, and Terminal closes by itself (about 10 seconds)
- You can also update any time: **your name → Update Mac app** (in the Mac app)
- The first time, your Mac may ask if Terminal can control Terminal — click **OK** (it’s how the window closes by itself)
