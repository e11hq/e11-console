## Mac and computer
- The task creator's comments have a coloured bar down their left side, so they stand out
- Content plans: the Open button sits at the right edge of each row
- Widget (Mac App Store app): Super Admins see the team's tasks, Account Managers their clients' tasks

## iPhone
- Menus, pickers and dates open as real iPhone sheets: people with photos and search, colours, emoji, priority numbers, a calendar for every date
- New task and Request amendments open as iPhone sheets; pull one down with something typed and it tucks away like a Mail draft, ready to continue
- A task in the Trash opens as an iPhone sheet, with Restore and file previews
- The task's Client opens a sheet with search
- Part done shows as a green ring; three-button rows stay on one line
- Notifications come as iPhone's own banners, also while the app is open, and none are missed if your phone was offline
- Widget: Super Admins see the team's tasks, Account Managers their clients' tasks
- Most of these come with the next app update
