## New
- Morning summary for Production and Social: at 10:30 am, Monday to Friday, a short note on how your day looks — due today, overdue, waiting for CD review and what's first up. Phones with notifications on get it as a notification; an open app shows it once. Switch it off for holidays in Me (phone) or My profile
- Uploads are much faster, and on the phone they carry on after you switch to another app and come back

## Phone
- Every pop-up (assignees, priority, menus…) slides up from the bottom and can be dragged down to close
- Touch and hold a comment for reactions, Reply, Edit, Copy text and Delete
- New comment box: a slim bar that opens into a sheet with the formatting tools and a round send button
- Swiping back shows the page you're going back to, sliding in underneath
- Filters stay on one line — swipe sideways to see them all
- Pop-up overlays dim the whole screen, including behind the time and battery, and page titles stay sharp
- For the full effect on iPhone, remove E11 Console from the Home Screen and add it again from Safari once

## Everywhere
- Picture and video previews keep their size while loading (with a soft shimmer), so comments no longer jump; on the phone they're at most 240px tall
- Comments no longer pull you back down to the newest one when you scroll up straight after opening them
- Editing a task description looks exactly like reading it
- The update pop-up is simpler, with full-size buttons, and shows "Reloading…" when you tap Reload
