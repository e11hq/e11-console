## Who sees what
- A task's Guardian has it on their Home / My Day, My Tasks and in search until the whole task is done, and sees it on its client's page
- Tasks you're @mentioned in (in the description, a comment or a subtask) show on that client's page, and the client is in your Clients list
- Each role's Clients list and a client's page show the same tasks as that person's own lists; an Account Manager with no clients yet sees none
- Social Executives see the tasks they're assigned to and the ones they made from a content plan; Social Managers also see every task made from a plan by their team

## Tasks
- CD review rounds: once files are sent, CD review stays on and the status is locked until the CD approves everything
- A completed task's due date can't be changed from the iPhone either
- What goes to the Trash now says Trash everywhere

## Notifications
- A task's Guardian and anyone @mentioned in its description hear about it as its assignees do
- Someone @mentioned in a comment hears the replies in that thread
- Opening a birthday notification shows their greeting

## Looks
- Client logos are shown whole, never cropped, on their own background
- The occasion greeting shows once per person, not once per device
- Initials skip a middle "Al" (Yousef Al Hwij → YH)

## Mac app
- Window → E11 Console (⌘0) brings the main window back after it's closed

## iPhone
- The @ list shows each person's role under their name
