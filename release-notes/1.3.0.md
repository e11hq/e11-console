## Expense claims
- Everyone (except Finance-only people) fills in a monthly expense claim on a computer: My profile → My reimbursements
- Group items by date and title, attach receipts, sign, and submit. Download the claim as a PDF with its receipts.
- From the 20th to the 27th, a reminder pops up once a day from 11:30 AM until you submit, say you have no claims, or say you submitted offline
- Claims for a month close on the 28th. From then on, new claims are for the next month.
- Finance sees every submitted claim under Reimbursements, marks it **HR approved** and then **Paid**, and the person gets an Inbox update at each step

## Finance
- **Download report** on Expenses (Admins, Finance Admins and Finance Viewers): pick one month or up to 12. You get a PDF of subscriptions, staff reimbursements and extra expenses, with each invoice's file name, or **Missing**.
- Tick **Include invoices and receipts** to get a .zip instead: the report PDF plus an Attachments folder, one folder per month, with the files named as in the report
- Add a missing invoice afterwards, for any earlier month: on Subscriptions, Extra expenses, and the Expenses lists
- From the 25th, Finance Admins get a daily reminder listing this month's subscriptions that don't have an invoice yet

## Announcements
- Admins can send an announcement from My profile, with a title and description
- Everyone sees it once as a pop-up, on computers and phones

## Everyone
- The light/dark button shows the mode you're in: a sun for light, a moon for dark, a half circle on Automatic
- On Home, Recent activity opens a mention or comment right on that comment, highlighted
- Checkboxes show the pointing-hand cursor
- On the phone, My profile no longer repeats the Appearance setting (it's on the Me tab)
