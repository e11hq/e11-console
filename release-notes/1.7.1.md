## Scheduled comments
- On a computer, the comment button now says Send (Reply for replies) and has a ⌄ next to it to schedule the comment for later
- Type a time like "tomorrow 2pm" or "in 3 hours", pick In 20 minutes, In 2 hours, Tomorrow, In 2 days or Next week, or choose a day on the calendar and then a time
- Times outside office hours (9 AM–6 PM, Monday–Friday) show a small note
- Until it's sent, only you see it in the task, with Edit, Send now and Cancel. Attached files show once it's sent
- It's sent on time even if your computer is off, and mentions and notifications go out then
- If the task is completed or deleted first, it isn't sent and you're told in your Inbox. You can get it back as a draft

## Unsent comments
- Leaving a task with a comment, reply or files not sent asks: Send comment, Keep as draft or Go back
- Drafts stay on your device even after closing the app
- Pressing Send while files are still uploading sends it once they finish
