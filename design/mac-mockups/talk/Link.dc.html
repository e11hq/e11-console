Link a Device
Point your iPhone at the code on your computer
Open E11 Talk on your computer. The code works once, for 2 minutes.
After the scan: the iPhone says "Linked — your chats are on it"; the Mac shows the startup screen with "Setting up your account" (board S2) while the iPhone hands over the last 90 days, then the Mac gets every new message itself, in step with the iPhone. Unlinked from the iPhone (Linked Devices › the Mac › Unlink), signed out of Talk on the iPhone, removed, or the iPhone not opening Talk for 30 days: the Mac is signed out and its chats removed — back to this screen.
