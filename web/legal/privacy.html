Privacy policy

E11 Console and E11 Talk · Last updated 14 November 2026

E11 Console and E11 Talk are the apps of E11 Agency, a creative agency in Dubai. E11 Console is the agency’s work app: tasks, clients, files, content plans and reviews. E11 Talk is its chat app, for the team and the clients it works with. Only people the agency adds can use them: its staff, the freelancers it works with and its finance team, and — in E11 Talk only — the client contacts the agency adds. There is no public sign-up, no advertising and no tracking.

Signing in

  • You sign in with your email and a password you set yourself. The first time, or if you forget your password, an E11 administrator gives you a 4-digit sign-in code in person or by message; it works once, only for your email, for 24 hours, and you then set a new password. Nothing is sent to you by email or text message.
  • Passwords and sign-in codes are kept only in a scrambled form (a hash), never as you typed them. The agency keeps a record of which administrator made a code for whom, and when; when a password is set with an administrator’s code, the other administrators are told.
  • Once signed in, the apps stay signed in on that device with a key of their own, and Face ID or Touch ID can sign you back in. That happens entirely on your device: the apps never see your face or fingerprint data.

What E11 Console keeps

  • Your account: your name, email, role and job title, set up by an E11 administrator; and, if you choose to add them, a profile photo, your birthday, the city you work from and your out-of-office dates.
  • Your work: the tasks, comments (including voice messages), reactions, files, pictures and videos you and your colleagues add, client folders, content plans, and review decisions.
  • Expense claims (if you use them): the amounts, the receipts you attach and your signature.
  • File links: if you copy a link to a file or folder, anyone you give that link to can open that file or folder, until it is deleted or its link is turned off.

What E11 Talk keeps

  • Your account: for the team, the same account as E11 Console. For a client contact: your name, email and the client you belong to, added by an E11 administrator. A client contact sees only the groups they are added to.
  • Your chats: the messages, photos, videos, voice messages, files, links, reactions and replies you send, and who has received and read them. They pass through E11 Agency’s server to the people in the group and are removed from the server once every one of their devices has them; anything not picked up is removed after 30 days. Groups with no new messages for 90 days are deleted.
  • On your devices: your chats are kept on your iPhone (and on a computer you link to it). Chat Backup saves them to your own iCloud account, which E11 Agency cannot see; you can set its Automatic Backup to Off in Settings › Chat Backup.
  • Linked computers: if you link a computer from Settings › Linked Devices, your iPhone hands it your recent chats and it then gets your new messages itself. It is signed out, and its chats removed, when you unlink it, sign out of E11 Talk on your iPhone, or don’t open E11 Talk on your iPhone for 30 days.

Both apps

  • Notifications: if you allow them, a notification address for your device, so your updates and messages reach it. A notification shows a short summary, such as who wrote in which group or commented on which task.
  • Your settings, such as the theme, notification choices or Auto-download, kept on your device or with your account.
  • The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo or video, or to scan the code that links a computer; the microphone only while you record a voice message or a video. Photos are saved to your library only when you choose to save them.

What they don’t do

  • No advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of your information.

Where it is kept

Everything is kept on E11 Agency’s own server in its Dubai office. A copy of the server’s data is kept for 14 days for backup. In E11 Console, deleted files stay in the trash for 30 days before they are removed for good, and read updates in the Inbox are cleared after 60 days.

Services the apps rely on

  • Cloudflare carries the connection to E11’s server and checks that you are signed in.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications to your devices, and iCloud holds your E11 Talk Chat Backup if you use it.

Neither receives your work or your chats for any other purpose.

Who can see what

In E11 Console, what you see and add is shared with your colleagues according to your role (for example, freelancers only see the clients they work on, and only the finance team sees finance records). In E11 Talk, messages are seen by the people in the group; a client contact sees only their own groups. E11 administrators manage the team and the client contacts.

Your choices

You can change your photo, birthday, city and out-of-office dates in E11 Console’s Me / My profile, and switch notifications off in either app. When you leave E11, or a client contact is removed, the account is switched off and signed out everywhere.
