Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

E11 Console is the internal work app of E11 Agency, a creative agency in Dubai. Only people the agency adds to its team can use it: staff, the freelancers it works with, and its finance team. There is no public sign-up, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app keeps

  • Your account: your name, work email, role and job title, set up by an E11 administrator; and, if you choose to add them, a profile photo, your birthday and the city you work from.
  • Your work: the tasks, comments, reactions, files, pictures and videos you and your colleagues add, client folders, content plans, and review decisions.
  • Expense claims (if you use them): the amounts, the receipts you attach and your signature.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a notification address for your device, so your updates reach it.
  • Your settings, such as the theme or the Face ID lock, kept on your device.

What it doesn’t do

  • No advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of your information.
  • Face ID happens entirely on your iPhone; the app never sees your face or fingerprint data.
  • The camera and microphone are used only when you choose to take a photo or video to attach; photos are saved to your library only when you choose to save a file.

Where it is kept

Everything is kept on E11 Agency’s own server in its Dubai office. A copy of the database is kept for 14 days for backup, and deleted files stay in the trash for 30 days before they are removed for good. Read updates in the Inbox are cleared after 60 days.

Services the app relies on

  • Cloudflare carries the connection to E11’s server and handles signing in (it sends the one-time sign-in code to your work email).
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications to your iPhone. A notification shows a short summary of the update, such as who commented on which task.

Neither receives your work for any other purpose.

Who can see what

What you see and add is shared with your colleagues according to your role (for example, freelancers only see the clients they work on, and only the finance team sees finance records). E11 administrators manage the team list.

Your choices

  • You can change your photo, birthday and city, and switch notifications off, in Me / My profile.
  • When you leave E11, your account is switched off. To have your account information deleted, or to ask what the app keeps about you, write to the address below.
