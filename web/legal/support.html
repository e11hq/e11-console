Quick answers

How do I sign in? Enter your email and your password. The first time, or if you’ve forgotten your password, ask an E11 administrator for a sign-in code: enter it, then set your own password. After that, Face ID or Touch ID can sign you back in. Signed in to E11 Console on your iPhone? E11 Talk signs you in as the same person.

I don’t have an account. Accounts are created by E11 administrators — the team on the Team page, client contacts in E11 Talk. Ask one to add you.

Notifications don’t arrive. In the app, check that notifications are on (E11 Console: Me; E11 Talk: Settings › Notifications); on your iPhone, check Settings › Notifications.

Face ID lock. In E11 Console, turn it on or off in Me. If Face ID fails, use your iPhone passcode.

Saving a file. Open the file and choose Download (E11 Console) or Save / Share (E11 Talk); the share sheet lets you save it to Files or Photos.

A new iPhone. In E11 Talk, turn on Settings › Chat Backup on your old iPhone; on the new one, sign in and restore from Chat Backup.
