  • Or ask in the office — we usually answer the same working day (Monday to Friday, Dubai time).

Quick answers

How do I sign in? Enter your E11 work email; you’ll get a one-time code by email. There’s no password to remember.

I don’t have an account. Accounts are created by E11 administrators — ask one to add you on the Team page.

Notifications don’t arrive. In the app, open Me and check that notifications are on; on your iPhone, check Settings › Notifications › E11 Console.

Face ID lock. Turn it on or off in Me. If Face ID fails, use your iPhone passcode.

Saving a file. Open the file and choose Download; the share sheet lets you save it to Files or Photos.
